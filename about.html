FIDA FILAMENTS PRIVATE LIMITED · 3RD GENERATION MANUFACTURER

Manufacturing confidence, metre by metre.

A third-generation, Nagercoil-based manufacturer serving customers across India and Asia with monofilament yarn engineered in all sizes — for fishing, industrial, decorative and agricultural markets — all possible sizes available.

A practical partner for materials that matter.

FIDA is a third-generation, carrying forward decades of filament-making knowledge into a modern manufacturing operation. We produce synthetic filaments, fishing nets, ropes and technical netting for distributors, retailers and industrial buyers across India and export markets throughout Asia.

Our material expertise spans nylon monofilament yarn (all sizes available) and fabric-type multifilament yarn, both used in fishing nets, fishing nets, durable industrial netting systems, and specialty yarn for decorative craft and agricultural use.
